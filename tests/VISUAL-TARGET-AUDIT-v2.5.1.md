## Year 2 Electricity comparison focus

`Nyalakan Mentol` is the closest direct comparison. The retained application uses a responsive DOM/CSS circuit simulator rather than the reference's rendered tabletop illustration. The alignment pass will therefore strengthen the mission header, stage rail, workbench rim, component labels, PICO anchoring, success explanation and action hierarchy while preserving the interactive circuit engine and mobile accessibility.

## Deliberate product differences

- No XP, stars, trophy economy, achievements, coins or scoring.
- No new curriculum, missions, progression rules or storage fields.
- No fixed desktop dashboard squeezed into portrait layouts.
- No reference image or heavy laboratory bitmap embedded in production.
- No replacement of existing PICO, circuit, light, mixture or year-specific production assets.
- Parent Zone and Buku Makmal remain quieter than gameplay.
